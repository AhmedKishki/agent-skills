# Checklist: wording and order

Part A continued, after A1 to A7 in [checklist.md](checklist.md). These items come from the author's own rules for plain writing. A8 to A10 run on the `## Sentences` section of the report, A11 on the `## Blocks` section.

**A8 Removable words.** A phrase or sentence whose deletion leaves the meaning unchanged.

- **A8a The commenting tail.** A trailing phrase that comments on the claim instead of adding to it. *a rounded prescaler would make every duration run fast or slow, with nothing to show for it.*
- **A8b The repeated meaning.** A word or clause that says again what its sentence already says.

Test: delete it and reread. If the text means the same, cut it.

**A9 Not the simplest word.** A longer, rarer or more formal noun or verb where a plain word says the same. *serves as* for *is*, *carries three assumptions* for *makes three assumptions*. Keep a technical term when it is the exact name of the thing.

Test: put the plainest word that keeps the meaning in its place. If the sentence still says the same, the original word fails.

**A10 Abstract without a real case.** A rule or claim left general where the subject offers an example, or an example the writer invented.

- **A10a The missing example.** *with an interrupt at each half of its range,* where the text could give the counts, 0 and 32,768.
- **A10b The invented example.** A case, figure or quotation that the sources, the code or the user's data do not contain.

Test: can the reader point at one real instance? If the document has no real example to give, the fix is a question to the author, never an invented case.

**A11 Order and grouping.** Ideas out of their logical order, or related ideas split apart.

- **A11a The early idea.** A block that needs an idea the text gives only later.
- **A11b The jump back.** A return to a finished topic that adds no new step.
- **A11c The split group.** Related ideas in places apart. Group by kind first, then by time, then by place, unless the domain sets another order. A CV groups entries by kind (education, work), then by date, then by place.

Test: write the topic of each block in order. A block that needs a later block, or a topic that appears in two places apart, fails.
