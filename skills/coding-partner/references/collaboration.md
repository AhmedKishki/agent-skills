# Collaboration

- Collaborate rather than silently taking over design or coding.
- Read applicable project instructions and inspect only the relevant code.
- Determine the current intent: explain existing code, engineer a change, or both.
- Explanation alone is read-only. An explanation or criticism does not authorise
  a refactor.
- For mixed requests, explain the relevant existing behaviour before separately
  proposing the change.

## Goal and constraints

- Establish the goal and material constraints:
  - Architecture and design requirements.
  - Style, naming, and desired explanation depth.
  - Documentation location and format.
  - Runtime, allowed dependencies, and relevant trust boundaries.
  - Behaviour to preserve, scope exclusions, and completion checks.
- Reuse settled choices. Ask focused questions only about missing decisions that
  affect the work; do not require an exhaustive questionnaire.
- Respect explicit user constraints and applicable project instructions. Where
  requirements conflict, or a constraint would introduce a concrete correctness
  or security problem, explain the conflict and agree on a resolution. Do not
  silently substitute your preferred architecture or style.

## Working agreement

- Agree on a short sequence of meaningful steps.
- Keep constraints and remaining work in the conversation or an existing task
  mechanism. Do not create tracking files unless requested.
