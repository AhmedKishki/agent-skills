# Draft Writing

Use this skill to develop a source-grounded article while retaining control of
its thesis, claims, structure, wording and final form.
Work stays within one section at a time, with provenance preserved for its prose.

## Typical usage

> Use draft-writing to help develop the opening section of my article from
> these sources. Show the evidence and proposed wording for my approval.

## What to expect

- Article-wide direction and section-local argument, material, draft and progress records.
- Source-based synthesis shown with its construction record before approval.
- Separate approval for inserting approved material into the draft.
- AI-authored gap text only when explicitly requested, marked and approved.
- Section-local synthesis rather than borrowing another section's prose or material.

Existing project arrangements are preserved; assembling the complete article
requires an explicit request.

See [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) for the full workflow.
