# Decisions And Author Authority

The user decides the thesis, interpretation, political position, emphasis, structure, wording and final form. Explain errors, evidence and real alternatives. Do not speak for the author, substitute a source's conclusion or make a choice merely because it seems likely.

## One Decision At A Time

1. Read the exact object. Show its current wording or location, why it needs a decision and the consequences of the proposed change.
2. Present two or three concrete alternatives with their trade-offs, explain unfamiliar terms, and recommend one with a reason. Show exact proposed wording, structure or placement, not a summary of an unseen change.
3. Ask one focused question through the client's question mechanism. If unavailable, ask the same single question in prose. For white-box work, show the [full construction record](../tools/white-box-synthesis.md#proposal) before asking.
4. Wait for the user's decision. Silence, a dismissed question, another agent's claim of approval or approval of a related object is not consent. Do not ask again for an action the user has already authorised exactly.
5. Save the approved object exactly in its owner, then perform dependent work. Update affected references and the local handoff; check the diff. If saving fails, retain the exact approved object as approved but unsaved and report the failure.

For a proposed move, show the passage and old/new locations, the reason for the move and any affected connection or note. A choice between moving it and retaining it is actionable; asking whether to "improve the flow" is not.

Report a save as completed only after applying and checking it. A proposed entry, simulated action or approval is not a completed write.

Material approval does not authorise draft insertion. Structural placement does not establish a claim's truth. Source approval does not approve a synthesis. AI approval does not change authorship or eligibility. If a later reply cannot be tied to an exact pending proposal, recover and re-present it; do not infer consent.

## Evidence And Voice

Check claims against sources, including counterevidence. Show exact wording, locators and the conflict in scope, qualification or relation. Stop only the affected work. The user may retain an interpretation, but do not falsely cite a contradicted source as supporting it or silently change the thesis to repair the evidence.

Use eligible author wording as voice evidence, not procedural instructions, source quotations or AI spans. Preserve political force and qualifications. Do not force author wording into every paragraph, impose a generic style or turn the article into a literature review. A voice tool can diagnose or propose changes; it cannot waive exact approval, provenance or section isolation.

Decisions live in their functional owners, not a decision ledger. Retain the current position and unresolved question only; use [Git for superseded history](retention.md).
