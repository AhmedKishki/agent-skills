# Predraft

Maintain one active `{project}-predraft.md` containing every exact passage the user has selected as raw material. Update it immediately after each selection.

The predraft is where the article's voice is assembled — and the voice is the user's. The user's own synthesis wording is authoritative and stays present; source material strengthens, grounds, and qualifies it. A passage that loses the user's voice has failed, however faithful to the sources.

Work on one arc paragraph at a time. Before discussing wording, require its arc line — proposition, place in the section chain, named source items — read with the section question and the article chain in view, plus a current relevant-source rescan. If either input is missing or stale, state whether the [article arc](article-arc.md) or [source review](source-review.md) is required and stop. Resolve questions and save selected wording before another paragraph. Only the user may suspend or switch; still pause at every required question or use decision.

Selection means **available as raw material**, not unchanged draft inclusion. Keep still-active alternatives until the user withdraws or supersedes them. Exclude questions, discussion, rationale, unselected candidates, raw synthesis inputs, intermediate construction traces, arc decisions, report findings, and draft-use history; retain the final white-box record as required provenance.

The predraft is the only deliberate collection of non-final article wording. Reproduce unchanged wording from thesis-and-vision, user-synthesis, source-map items, white-box synthesis (terminal Mixed), or AI wording only after the user selects that exact passage for predraft use, citing its canonical basis. For an AI gap suggestion, cite the dated exchange, any exact Human lexical bases, named active sources and coverage checked, and any inaccessible or partial sources. Predraft grouping decides neither final order nor final paragraph form; the approved predraft report governs global treatments.

Apply the role rules in [source-maps.md](source-maps.md): MT-only items may govern passage fit but never supply predraft wording. Exclude framework exposition, abstract definitions, and loose metatheory vocabulary introduced only to signal alignment; ask if role, fit, or visibility is uncertain.

The file is two parts: Part 1 holds every selected passage as prose under its heading — exact paragraph ID and readable title, or neutral **Passage N** — with no process metadata; Part 2 holds each passage's production record under the same heading. Use the Human default only for Human passages. A substantive heading shares its passage provenance; **Passage N** is a fixed process label reserved for material without an arc paragraph. Give each Mixed/AI passage a full local **Provenance** stamp in its Part 2 record with compact references to every original Human input. For white-box synthesis, the Part 2 record retains the final record: Input keys, span map, Ordering, Cuts, Argument path, Metatheory fit, and Verification. Never correct a selected white-box passage in place; rerun the method from its original Human bases and obtain new selection and removal authority for the replacement passage and record. Selection never waives fidelity.

Individual passage selections do not approve the complete predraft. When every active arc paragraph has raw material, show the entire predraft and its coverage. Only explicit user approval sets the complete file to **Approved** for whole-predraft review. Approval means the set is ready for global diagnosis, not that any passage is final prose or may be silently changed.

Any added, replaced, withdrawn, or corrected passage returns the predraft to **Working** and invalidates the current predraft report and draft after the required removal-impact notice. Reapprove the complete predraft before a new [predraft report](predraft-report.md) may be requested.
