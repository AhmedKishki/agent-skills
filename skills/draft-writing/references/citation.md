# Citation

## Purpose

Verify and cite claims in a finalised article without changing its prose.

## Precondition

Begin only from an immutable `{project}-draft-vN.md` approved as finalised. Load its approved article arc, argument bases, source maps, accessible originals and draft direction.

## Procedure

1. Identify a claim needing support and its exact draft location.
2. Check the original source, locator, scope, date, modality and qualifications.
3. If support fails, report the defect and return the claim to its owner. Do not rewrite finalised prose inside citation work.
4. Propose the exact footnote and marker location in `{project}-citations.md`.
5. Apply only approved notes and markers.
6. Validate every marker, note, source and locator.
7. Present the complete cited article for approval and save it as `{project}-cited-draft-vN.md`.

## Citation-item format

```markdown
# Citations

**Source draft:** project-draft-v1.md

## CIT-001 — Short claim name

**Status:** Proposed

### Draft location

Section 1, paragraph 3, after “commodity fetishism”.

### Claim

<Claim requiring support.>

### Source and location

- AN24
- Printed p. 47

### Support and limits

<Why it supports the claim and any limit.>

### Proposed footnote

Karl Marx, ...
```

## Style

Follow the citation style recorded in draft direction. Never assume a house style that the user has not supplied or approved.

## Completion

Every claim requiring support has an approved verified note or an explicit approved reason for none, and cited prose exactly matches the finalised source draft.
