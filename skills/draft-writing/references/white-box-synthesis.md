# White-box synthesis

## Inputs

Use exact approved Human or Mixed inputs: source-map excerpts, user wording at its destination, or approved outputs with finite, recoverable provenance. Identify inputs by stable labels and locations. Source wording remains source-authored.

Include relevant user wording unless the user authorises its exclusion. Do not force unrelated wording into a passage. Source-only synthesis is valid when no relevant user wording exists.

Approval does not turn Mixed into Human. An output using Mixed input remains Mixed. Explicitly AI-authored or `⟦AI-AUTHORED: …⟧` spans are permanently ineligible, even after approval. Exclude those spans and relations that depend on them; do not reject all Mixed material. Unverified inputs remain unavailable until verified and approved.

## Operations

- **COPY:** copy a contiguous span.
- **DELETE:** remove words without changing subject, scope, modality, qualification or relation.
- **ORDER:** arrange spans only where an authorised input establishes their relation.
- **INFLECT:** change tense, number, grammatical case, article or an unambiguous pronoun.
- **NORMALISE:** make meaning-neutral changes to spelling, capitalisation, typography or punctuation.

No operation may invent a connection, cause, comparison, interpretation, qualification or conclusion.

## Proposal

1. Identify the planned passage, its purpose and exact inputs.
2. Check input approval, provenance, context and evidence limits.
3. Construct the passage and verify every meaning-bearing word and relation.
4. Present the complete output, its provenance and a construction record for every sentence.
5. Obtain approval of the exact output before saving it directly in its section material.

For each output sentence, show:

- The exact output sentence.
- Each input label, author, source and locator. For user wording, identify the user and its local location.
- The exact copied span from each input.
- Every operation, including deleted words, normalisation and the basis for ordering spans.

Codes, operation counts and summaries do not replace this record. Keep construction records in the approval proposal. Save only the basis references, provenance and live constraints with the passage; retain exact local inputs needed to reconstruct it.

## Gaps

Show the smallest missing wording, evidence, relation or authorisation and explain why the permitted operations cannot supply it. Ask the user to resolve it. Do not invent or volunteer AI wording.

Only an explicit request permits an AI-authored suggestion outside synthesis. Mark its exact span `⟦AI-AUTHORED: exact wording⟧`, seek exact approval and preserve its authorship. It may enter a draft, never section material or later white-box inputs. A request for smoother prose does not authorise a new substantive claim.
