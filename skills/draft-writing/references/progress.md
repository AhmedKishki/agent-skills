# Progress

`{project}-progress.md` is the only session handoff. Keep:

- The active section and passage, with exact file references.
- Whether its proposal is pending, approved but unsaved, or saved.
- The next action and current draft or footnote work needed to resume.
- Live blockers and verification limits, with links to their owners.
- Unfinished source reading and the next unused IDs or codes.

Do not copy passage text, the plan, settled construction records or completed activity logs here. Keep only state that changes what the next session should do.

Use stable source, excerpt, passage and local-input labels. Allocate IDs monotonically; never reuse a removed, rejected or retired ID. Keep only namespaces in use; new projects need no argument IDs. Check history when the next value is uncertain.

Update the handoff after a decision, save, gap or change of focus. Remove resolved blockers without lowering counters. Re-present an exact pending proposal before interpreting a later reply as its approval.

Before ending, check that another session can find the current object, its approval state, its blockers and the first action without relying on chat history.
