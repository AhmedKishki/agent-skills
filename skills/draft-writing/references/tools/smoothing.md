# Smoothing

Smoothing reduces repetition across passages or paragraphs within one section. It combines related parts, keeps distinct arguments and evidence, and repairs sequence using [white-box operations](white-box-synthesis.md). It is not permission to generate new wording, impose a generic style or work across sections.

Read the local arc, relevant material and draft passages, requirements and live constraints. Name the owner being amended: material or draft. A change to one does not silently change the other.

1. Diagnose repeated claims, scattered explanations, broken transitions and misplaced passages. State each passage's distinct function before proposing a deletion.
2. Propose one bounded amendment. Show the exact before-and-after text and location, with the complete white-box construction record for every transformed eligible sentence.
3. Explain what is retained, moved, combined or removed. Show that no necessary evidence, scope, qualification or distinct argument is lost. Prefer the more developed account when it preserves those functions; do not delete purposeful repetition without approval.
4. Follow [decisions](../rules/decisions.md). Apply the exact amendment immediately after approval, then check paragraph development, basis references and affected footnotes. Update local progress before the next amendment.

For example, if one paragraph defines a term and another repeats the definition before supplying a qualification, propose keeping the definition once and retaining the qualification. Show both original spans, the proposed order and its authorised relation; do not invent a transition merely to join them.

If approved wording cannot supply a coherent transition, expose the gap and use the gap procedure. A request for smoother prose does not authorise an AI suggestion or a new substantive claim.

Approved AI spans remain marked. They cannot be transformed through white-box operations. Show any proposed deletion or relocation of an unchanged AI span separately as a placement decision, preserving its authorship and approved purpose. A new or altered AI span needs a fresh [AI-authored gap proposal](ai-authored.md) and exact approval, not reuse as an eligible input.

After an approved replacement, use [retention](../rules/retention.md). Do not accumulate alternative paragraph versions or completed amendment logs in material, drafts or progress.
