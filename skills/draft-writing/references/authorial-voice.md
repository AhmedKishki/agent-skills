# Authorial voice

## Purpose

Preserve and help the user develop their recognisable thought, language, argumentative character and political force throughout the workflow.

## Authority

Apply [ownership and routing](ownership-and-routing.md), [consent and state](consent-and-state.md), [user wording](user-wording.md), [provenance](provenance.md), and [editing and reference integrity](editing-and-reference-integrity.md). The user determines the voice. Project-specific requirements belong in thesis-and-vision; exact reusable language belongs in user wording. Create no independent voice profile.

## Voice basis

Use only operational user wording, direct user answers and corrections, exact user-approved article prose, and operational voice requirements in thesis-and-vision. Do not infer voice from source authors, publication stereotypes, generic polished prose or AI summaries. Treat any description of the user's voice as a proposal until the user confirms it.

## Procedure

1. Identify the exact basis relevant to the passage or section: vocabulary, directness, rhythm, argumentative movement, political force, modality, qualifications and purposeful repetition.
2. When wording, emphasis or intended force is unclear, quote the affected material and ask one focused question that helps the user articulate it.
3. Prefer exact user wording, then approved prose. Keep the user as the speaking and arguing subject; source language supports rather than governs the argument.
4. Ask the user to develop missing language before offering AI wording. When requested, present one exact bounded proposal, identify its basis and offer to retain the original. Apply it only after explicit approval.
5. Report any departure with its exact location, effect and required decision. Diagnosis authorises no revision.

Do not replace distinctive language, irregularity or political force with generic fluency; add formulaic transitions, empty signposting, rhetorical symmetry or metaphors; imitate a writer or publication; let source vocabulary displace the user's concepts; flatten sentence rhythm; remove repetition before deciding its function; alter a position for smoothness; or attribute AI or source wording to the user.

## Validation

Check that the user remains the speaking and arguing subject; characteristic terms, distinctions, rhythm and directness remain recognisable; political force, modality and qualifications remain; concrete relations have not become generic abstractions; transitions express approved relations; source language remains subordinate; and every AI-authored span is exact, bounded, disclosed and approved.

## Completion condition

The prose remains continuous with its approved voice basis, and every departure is supplied by the user or approved as an exact proposal.

## Blocking condition

Unclear intended force, competing bases, missing user language or a stylistic change that may alter meaning blocks the affected prose and dependent work.
