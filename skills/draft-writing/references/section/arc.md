# Section Arc

Keep that section's driving claim or question and internal development in `sections/section-n/arc-section-n.md`. Read the article direction, its brief purpose in `plan.md`, and the section's current progress. Link to the article purpose instead of repeating it.

```markdown
# Section 1 Arc

**Article purpose:** [Plan](../../plan.md#article-order)
**Driving question:** <The question this section answers.>

## P-001
<Short descriptive title.>

**Purpose:** <What this passage establishes and adds to the section.>
**Connection:** <The authorised relation to the next passage.>
**Evidence needed:** <The specific unresolved evidence need.>
**Material:** [MAT-001](material-section-1.md#mat-001)
```

Use `P-nnn` headings in reading order. Allocate IDs through [section progress](progress.md); never change an ID merely to change order. Omit connections, evidence needs and material links when not yet available rather than inventing them. A question may stand without an approved answer.

Develop consequential choices through [decisions](../rules/decisions.md). Explain each passage's distinct purpose. Check how one passage prepares the next; sequence alone cannot establish cause, comparison or interpretation. Ask for a missing relation rather than supplying one silently.

Keep exact inputs and approved prose in material, source quotations in maps, and approval/task state in progress. Material-file order does not determine draft order. Do not add an intermediate argument file or copy another section's outline into this arc.

After approval, save the exact structural change, update affected local links, and identify any draft amendment it requires. Structure approval does not approve that amendment; propose it separately. Finish by checking that each planned passage advances the section and that any remaining connection gap is explicit.
