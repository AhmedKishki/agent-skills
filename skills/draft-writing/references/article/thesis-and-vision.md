# Thesis And Vision

**Owner:** `thesis-and-vision.md`, at `organisation/thesis-and-vision.md` in the default new-project layout.
**Allowed:** Motivation, central claim, theoretical and explanatory commitments, scope, their qualifications, and unresolved questions that can change the thesis.
**Excluded:** Tone and citation requirements (requirements); section order (plan); passage order (arc); exact inputs (material); source quotations and source-specific limits (source maps); task state, counters and activity history.

Read the user's exact direction and the current file. Follow [decisions](../rules/decisions.md) before changing a substantive position. Use these headings only where content exists:

```markdown
# Thesis And Vision

## Motivation
<Why the author is writing this article.>

## Main Thesis
<The author's current central claim.>

## Commitments
<Theoretical and explanatory commitments, with their limits.>

## Scope
<What the article addresses and what it does not.>

## Open Questions
<Only unresolved questions that can change the thesis.>
```

Before selecting evidence, identify what the planned passage must explain, demonstrate, qualify or challenge, how it serves the thesis, and what could contradict it. Do not collect only confirming evidence or force sources into the author's position.

If evidence conflicts with the thesis, show the exact conflict and ask the author to resolve it. Save only the approved current position. Identify affected section arcs and uses; record unfinished reviews in their local progress files without silently rewriting them.

Article direction governs synthesis but is not automatically eligible prose. Request or identify approved local author wording for the passage rather than treating instructions as article sentences. At completion, check that requirements and section outlines remain in their own owners and that no superseded thesis remains operative.
