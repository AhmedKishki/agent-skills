# Draft direction

## Purpose

Record how the article should be written in `{project}-draft-direction.md`.

## Contains

- Audience and publication.
- Length and format.
- Language and citation style.
- Voice requirements.
- Desired emphasis, pacing and theoretical density.
- Rules for examples, headings, conclusions and other formal choices.

## Excludes

- The article's thesis.
- Source evidence.
- Argument claims.
- Article order.
- Reader-facing draft prose.

## Procedure

1. Route writing requirements here when they govern form rather than substance.
2. Use descriptive headings instead of a chronological log.
3. Preserve a replaced requirement under `## Superseded direction` only when later work still needs its history.
4. Report affected arc or draft units when a requirement changes.

## Format

```markdown
# Draft direction

> **Editing note:** You may edit this file directly. Put each requirement under the heading it governs.

## Audience and publication

<Requirements or None.>

## Length and form

- <Requirement>

## Voice and language

- <Requirement>

## Development and emphasis

- <Requirement>

## Citation

- <Requirement>

## Superseded direction

- None.
```

## Completion

Every supplied writing requirement is recorded once and no substantive thesis or article structure is stored here.
