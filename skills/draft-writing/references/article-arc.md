# Global and section article arc

Build from the approved thesis-and-vision and whichever active source maps, user-synthesis, and selected predraft material are currently available and valid. Begin with approved main questions. Add one only when the user agrees it is needed to establish, test, qualify, or conclude the thesis; sources may reveal the need but cannot expand scope. Leave fields depending on unavailable material **Undecided**. Article-arc and predraft may alternate at the user's direction; neither complete artifact is a prerequisite for beginning the other.

The arc has two scales. The **global arc** controls section sequence, questions, goals, thesis contributions, boundaries, handoffs, points of emphasis, and sole homes for claims or lists. Each **section arc** is that section's ordered series of **moves**: one move per distinct argument step, each carrying its proposition, role, position in the handoff chain, emphasis, boundary, and named source contributions. A move is the exact unit of synthesis: [white box synthesis](white-box-synthesis.md) runs once per move and takes its target from that move's row, so the position in the arc states exactly what is synthesised. Moves guide predrafting; they are not final paragraphs. Global drafting may combine approved move material only through the report-governed process in [drafting](drafting.md).

Every move makes one distinct contribution and every section serves a question and the thesis. Remove filler, loops, excess scope, or repeated claims outside their sole home only through the [removal-impact protocol](file-output-standard.md). An intentional repetition must have a distinct function and exact user authority.

Number the sections in reading order (1, 2, …, including any Preface and Conclusion) and the moves sequentially within their section: move **2.3** is the third move of section 2. IDs are stable—never renumber or reuse one once downstream work names it. An inserted section or move takes the next unused number; the global arc's sequence, not the number, carries reading order.

Structural fields require exact user wording or an exact selected Mixed/AI process proposal. Sources establish contributions, not structure. Leave unsupported fields **Undecided** and ask. Each claimed source contribution needs an active item and states its Article role and function—claim, mechanism, evidence, example, qualification, limit, or contest. If support or its rescan is missing/stale, state that [source review](source-review.md) is required and stop. Cross-source relations require direct source/user Human support or an explicitly selected, labelled post-rescan AI gap suggestion whose unsupported status, search coverage, and exact use remain visible.

Apply metatheory fit silently; Framework-only material is not an article contribution. Use one full-name source key; global rows aggregate contributions and move rows name items. Copy no source passages, synthesis traces, or separate matrices.

Treat the article title and every reader-facing heading as separate predraft material with named placements and raw-material decisions. Arc-only structural labels—section names and move titles—are not article wording.

In combined cells use these labels exactly: **Raised / Resolved**; **Goal / Motivation / Thesis contribution**; **Primary emphasis / Sole home**; **Include / Exclude**; globally **Previous handoff / Entry / Exit / Next handoff**; per move **Role / Section contribution**, **Builds from / Exit-handoff / Prepares next**, and **Synthesis / Use**. A row is **Undecided** until every required cell is set and **Resolved** only when every field is set and each Mixed/AI field has exact-field assent.

A move's **Position** chain gives its synthesis target's entry and exit: a section's first move builds from the previous section's exit handoff, and its last move prepares the next section's entry. One move row—its proposition, role, position, emphasis, and boundary, with its named source items—is exactly what one white-box run may realise; the run may not borrow a neighbouring move's proposition, handoff, or sources.

The Human declaration covers every Human cell and substantive label. One row-level basis covers shared Human fields; stamp only Mixed/AI exceptions. Resolve a move only from a current relevant-source rescan and active-map roles; otherwise state that [source review](source-review.md) is required and stop. Keep undecided cells, not questions.

Set **Use as raw material** only after the exact predraft passage exists and the user selects it for that move. This selection neither promises unchanged draft text nor authorises consolidation, reordering, omission, or new wording. Every active predraft passage or alternative must appear with its own **Use as raw material**, **Omit**, or **Undecided** disposition; plural passage references are allowed. The complete arc becomes **Approved** only after both scales, every raw-material disposition, and all authorities resolve and the user explicitly approves the complete file for whole-predraft review.

## Converting a pre-7.5 arc

An arc written before the moves model converts without new content authority: fold each argument unit's fields into its ordered moves—a unit with one step becomes one move—preserving every disposition, emphasis, sole home, handoff, and authority, and number sections and moves by the established reading order. Disclose exactly what the old local-arc tables and any separate moves list cease to govern, follow the [removal-impact protocol](file-output-standard.md), and retire the old representation; never keep it alongside the section arcs.
