# Article arc

## Purpose

Arrange approved arguments into the article's single structural plan in `{project}-article-arc.md`.

The article arc is the sole authority for article order, sections, passages, paragraph purposes, connections, inclusion, omission and recurrence. No separate blueprint or architectural-decision file is active.

## Procedure

1. Begin when enough approved argument drafts exist to decide the current structural question without guessing.
2. Develop the overall movement with the user one consequential decision at a time.
3. State real alternatives and their consequences. Record only approved decisions.
4. Add sections, passages and paragraph purposes as the movement becomes precise.
5. Assign every used argument draft and give every active argument one explicit treatment: include, combine, divide, condense, recur, reserve or omit.
6. Record required connections where they are implemented, not in a separate relation registry.
7. Keep unresolved questions beneath the unit they block.
8. Present the complete arc for approval when drafting can proceed without structural invention.
9. Save an approved immutable `{project}-article-arc-vN.md` before drafting against it.

If drafting exposes a structural problem, return here, revise the affected unit, obtain approval and create a new arc version. Invalidate only dependent draft prose.
