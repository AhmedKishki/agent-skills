# Planning

## Establish the subject

- Accept a book, concept, material, or subject as the learning subject.
- Ask when the subject, intended scope, or specified material is ambiguous.
- Honour the user's stated depth, goals, source limits, and sequence.
- Ask about an unspecified choice when it is necessary to proceed.
- Propose the topic divisions for confirmation instead of treating them as settled.
- Inspect enough of the sources to ground the plan in their actual content.

## Build the plan

- Divide the subject into topics and each topic into related subtopics.
- Treat one topic as one session, even when it spans several conversation turns.
- Group related arguments, ideas, or claims within that session.
- For a book or bounded material, map its arguments and preserve their dependencies.
- For a concept or wider subject, propose a coherent source-grounded sequence.
- Make the planned coverage explicit; do not claim completeness beyond it.
- Assign session numbers in the proposed teaching order, starting with 1.
- Map the sources and relevant locations to each session.

## Present and confirm

Begin the learning summary with: `Proposed plan: N sessions.`
Then show:

| Session | Topic | Related subtopics / arguments | Sources and locations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Topic] | [Ordered subtopics] | [Inspected sources] |

Explain the scope and why the sequence supports understanding, briefly.
Ask the user to confirm or adapt the plan. Do not start teaching yet.
If the user requests changes, revise the plan and obtain confirmation.
Treat an explicit instruction to begin the presented plan as confirmation.
Record the confirmed plan and any subsequent agreed changes in `project/plan.md`.

## Adapt and resume

- Answer questions within the current topic without automatically changing the plan.
- Propose a plan change if a question requires expanding or restructuring coverage.
- Obtain confirmation before applying that change or teaching the added coverage.
- Update the session count when an agreed change adds or removes topics.
- Preserve existing session numbers and records; do not silently renumber them.
- On resumption, read the saved plan and current session to locate the next subtopic.
- Ask if the saved position or the user's requested continuation is ambiguous.
