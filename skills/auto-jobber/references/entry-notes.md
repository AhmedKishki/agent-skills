# Retain context linked to factual entries

## Agree the structure

Keep applicant comments and extra context linked to the relevant factual entry in the applicant's maintained records. Use any agreed equivalent structure: this skill prescribes no filename, JSON schema or native-helper field. Notes should point to facts rather than duplicate the career database.

Make each link understandable to the applicant with human-readable section and entry/field selectors. Retain the comment, its author and its source locator, such as the conversation or supplied artifact. Preserve raw applicant wording separately from paraphrases or proposed prose. If a record moves or changes, review its selectors so the note does not silently attach to a different entry.

## Review what a note can support

Treat notes as candidate source input, not automatic verification. When a note supplies a new factual claim or correction, review its source and promote the supported claim to the factual records before using it as qualification evidence. Follow the selected workflow's evidence and currentness checks; merely saving a note does not satisfy them.

Distinguish applicant statements, speculation, agent-written suggestions and applicant-approved revisions. Speculation and agent suggestions cannot become applicant facts or voice. Approval of generated wording does not change its origin into raw applicant input; retain both authorship and the scope of approval.

## Select context for the document

Consult relevant existing notes before asking the applicant to repeat context. Use only material that serves the current document, and leave optional context out of the CV when it does not belong there. A cover letter can draw on an entry's relevant applicant context without inserting that context into the CV.

Follow Voice for current-job intent and wording. Do not invent motives or blindly recycle another job's motivation. If native wording snapshots or application helpers are selected, satisfy their actual input, provenance, snapshot and approval contracts; a note store does not replace those gates.

Keep private notes in the agreed applicant workspace, never in the reusable skill package or public employer research.
