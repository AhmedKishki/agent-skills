# Stage 2: collaborative source review

Work source by source from approved thesis-and-vision. Read the whole accessible text or record the exact access limit; assign the tracker's next [source-map](source-maps.md) code.

For each source, follow [source-maps.md](source-maps.md) to:

1. map every main argument/move, used or unused;
2. retain each directly supportive, qualifying, or contesting claim, argument, concept, conclusion, or requested example as a minimal one-topic exact excerpt;
3. prepare separate Article and Framework roles, source/item qualifications, coverage, and provenance;
4. show the user the full source name, readable excerpt/role, uncertainty, and any removal impact, then resolve any authority required by the substantive-change rules; and
5. save each authorised net-new item once and immediately refresh the [index](index.md) before the next question or action.

Complete each source's selection, path, coverage, roles, and qualifications independently. Within a source, keep only repetition that adds a distinct claim, relation, scope, modality, qualification, role, function, or materially useful occurrence; across sources, retain overlapping support independently because its provenance is distinct. Only Article-role items feed synthesis; Framework-only items silently test fit. Apply operative metatheory without force-fitting, treating it as article evidence, or importing its vocabulary. Ask when selection, role, relation, or fit is unclear.

Create no registry, summary, or evidence map. A source that qualifies, contests, or may strengthen the thesis triggers a plain-language question and any accepted revision returns to Stage 1. Establish a cross-source relation only from direct source/user Human support; completed white box synthesis may assemble but never supply that support. A labelled post-rescan AI gap suggestion may expose an unresolved proposed relation but never establish it.

Stage 2 ends when every active source map and role is resolved and every active excerpt resolves in the refreshed index. Framework-changing material returns to Stage 1. Before predrafting each argument, use source paths and the index to rescan every plausibly relevant original; also rescan after a missing step, requested example, vision change, or user request. Append only excerpts that are newly relevant and net-new within their source, refresh the index immediately, and reopen only affected work. Process one argument at a time.
