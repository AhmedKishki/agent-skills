## Six-step method

1. **Collect:** place the related passages together.
2. **Standardise:** align tense and inflection; choose consistent vocabulary only when Human material establishes equivalence.
3. **Combine:** place passages mechanically one after another.
4. **Reorder:** group related fragments without implying a new relation.
5. **Cut:** delete only repetition with the same subject, claim, scope, modality, and qualification.
6. **Repeat and verify:** repeat Steps 2–5; map every word/relation and test applicable fields—body role/entry/exit/boundary; title/heading placement/purpose/boundary; or thesis/vision/process function/purpose/boundary. If anything fails, stop and rescan or ask.

Add no lexical wording, change no source meaning or qualification, and never alter a quotation.
