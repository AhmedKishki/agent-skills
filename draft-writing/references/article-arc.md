# Global and local article arc

Build from the approved thesis-and-vision and whichever active source maps, user-synthesis, and selected predraft material are currently available and valid. Begin with approved main questions. Add one only when the user agrees it is needed to establish, test, qualify, or conclude the thesis; sources may reveal the need but cannot expand scope. Leave fields depending on unavailable material **Undecided**. Article-arc and predraft may alternate at the user's direction; neither complete artifact is a prerequisite for beginning the other.

The **global arc** controls section sequence, questions, goals, thesis contributions, boundaries, handoffs, points of emphasis, and sole homes for claims or lists. The **local arcs** define one argument unit at a time: its proposition, role, boundary, movement, sources, emphasis, and raw-material need. Local units guide predrafting; they are not immutable final paragraphs. Global drafting may combine approved local material only through the report-governed process in [drafting](drafting.md).

Every local unit makes one distinct contribution and every section serves a question and the thesis. Remove filler, loops, excess scope, or repeated claims outside their sole home only through the [removal-impact protocol](file-output-standard.md). An intentional repetition must have a distinct function and exact user authority.

Structural fields require exact user wording or an exact selected Mixed/AI process proposal. Sources establish contributions, not structure. Leave unsupported fields **Undecided** and ask. Each claimed source contribution needs an active item and states its Article role and function—claim, mechanism, evidence, example, qualification, limit, or contest. If support or its rescan is missing/stale, name **map-source** as the required input and return control. Cross-source relations require direct source/user Human support or an explicitly selected, labelled post-rescan AI gap suggestion whose unsupported status, search coverage, and exact use remain visible.

Apply metatheory fit silently; Framework-only material is not an article contribution. Use one full-name source key; global rows aggregate contributions and local rows name items. Copy no source passages, synthesis traces, or separate matrices.

Treat the article title and every reader-facing heading as separate predraft material with named placements and raw-material decisions. Arc-only structural labels are not article wording.

In combined cells use these labels exactly: **Raised / Resolved**; **Goal / Motivation / Thesis contribution**; **Primary emphasis / Sole home**; **Include / Exclude**; globally **Previous handoff / Entry / Exit / Next handoff**; locally **Role / Section contribution**, **Builds from / Exit-handoff / Prepares next**, and **Predraft material / Use**. A row is **Undecided** until every required cell is set and **Resolved** only when every field is set and each Mixed/AI field has exact-field assent.

The Human declaration covers every Human cell and substantive label. One row-level basis covers shared Human fields; stamp only Mixed/AI exceptions. Resolve a local argument only from a current relevant-source rescan and active-map roles; otherwise name **map-source** and return control. Keep undecided cells, not questions.

Set **Use as raw material** only after the exact predraft passage exists and the user selects it for that local function. This selection neither promises unchanged draft text nor authorises consolidation, reordering, omission, or new wording. Every active predraft passage or alternative must appear with its own **Use as raw material**, **Omit**, or **Undecided** disposition; plural passage references are allowed. The complete arc becomes **Approved** only after both scales, every raw-material disposition, and all authorities resolve and the user explicitly approves the complete file for whole-predraft review.
